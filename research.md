<h1>Research</h1><p class="lead">My research focuses on spatial flow, interpretable machine learning, and methods for understanding spatial and nonlinear effects.</p>
<h2>Spatial Flow</h2><p>I study statistical and machine-learning methods for flow data, with an emphasis on modeling and interpreting relationships between origins, destinations, and spatial structure.</p>
<h2>Interpretable Machine Learning</h2><p>My current methodological work explores explainable machine learning approaches that can quantify nonlinear contributions and make model outputs more interpretable.</p>
<h2>Spatial Effects</h2><p>I am interested in extracting spatial effects and examining spatial heterogeneity and association in geographic data, including environmental and urban applications.</p>
<h2>Current Direction</h2><p>My current projects include FLOW-GeoSHAP / Flow-GeoShapley and simultaneous parameter estimation for flow data, alongside work on nonlinear and mediated effects in intensive longitudinal data.</p>
